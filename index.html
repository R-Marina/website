// FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with small companies?

Yes. Teravane is focused on small and mid-sized businesses without an in-house IT team, or teams that need extra expertise for a specific project.

Do you work remotely?

Most of our work is done remotely through secure access to your systems. We can also work on-site when needed.

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on scope: smaller projects take 1–2 weeks, larger migrations 1–3 months. You'll get an exact timeline in our proposal.

Will you sign an NDA?

Of course. Confidentiality is a standard part of every engagement.

How does billing work?

We invoice monthly or per project milestone, in EUR or your preferred currency.
